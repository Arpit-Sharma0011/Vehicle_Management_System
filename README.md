# 🚗 Vehicle Management System

A comprehensive and efficient application designed to streamline the administration, tracking, and scheduling of vehicle fleets. This system automates daily operations, eliminates manual paperwork, and optimizes resource utilization for logistics and fleet management.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

*   **Vehicle Inventory:** Track vehicle details, registration status, models, and availability in real time.
*   **Driver Allocation:** Manage driver profiles, licenses, and assign drivers to specific vehicles or routes.
*   **Booking & Logs:** Schedule and log vehicle trips, preventing double-bookings and scheduling conflicts.
*   **Maintenance Records:** Monitor vehicle health, log service history, and track upcoming repair schedules.
*   **Fuel & Expense Tracking:** Log fuel costs and operational expenses to keep fleet budgets optimized.
*   **Role-Based Access Control:** Secure authentication system with separate dashboards for Admins and Standard Users.
